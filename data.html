Column Notes

  • max_temperature_C: Maximum temperature in Celsius.


  • pressure_kPa: Pressure in kilopascals.


  • oxygen_percent: Oxygen concentration in percent.


  • average_airflow_m_per_s: Average airflow in metres per second.


  • flame_spread_cm_per_min: Flame spread converted to cm/min.


  • burn_duration_seconds: Burn duration converted to seconds.


  • thickness_mm: Material thickness in millimetres.


  • density_kg_per_m3: Material density in kg/m³.


  • sensor_reading_count: Number of sensor readings recorded.


  • raw_sensor_readings_json: Original sensor readings.

Unit Conversions

  • Fahrenheit to Celsius: (F - 32) × 5/9


  • Feet/min to m/s: ft/min × 0.00508


  • Inches/min to cm/min: in/min × 2.54


  • Minutes to seconds: minutes × 60


  • Inches to mm: inches × 25.4


  • g/cm³ to kg/m³: g/cm³ × 1000
