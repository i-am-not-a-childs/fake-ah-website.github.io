Science notes

What should you look for?

Patterns

Look for values that increase or decrease together. A pattern in a practice graph is a starting point for asking questions.

Outliers

Look for points that are far away from the others. Check the observation before deciding what it means.

Units matter

Each measurement has its own unit. The graph legend, axis titles, and hover information identify the variables and units.

This educational project is not official NASA fire-safety guidance. Graphs show relationships in the displayed practice data only.
